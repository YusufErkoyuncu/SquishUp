Support

Help with gameplay, ads, privacy, and leaderboards

Before contacting support

Update Squish Up and your device, confirm your internet connection for ads or leaderboards, then restart the Game and device.

Leaderboards

Game Center on iOS and Google Play Games on Android are optional. Scores require the applicable service, an internet connection, and successful sign-in. Your local best score does not depend on leaderboard availability.

Advertising and rewards

Optional rewarded ads are offered for Continue, Double Reward, and Skin Trial. A reward is granted only after the advertising provider confirms completion. Interstitials appear only at eligible breaks and are frequency limited.

Custom Photo

The system photo picker lets you choose an image without granting broad photo-library access. Squish Up stores only a processed app-private copy. You can replace or reset it from the Game.

Privacy

Read the Squish Up Privacy Policy for information about local data, advertising, consent, and game services.

Contact

Email eksenworks@gmail.com with the app version, device model, operating-system version, and a short description. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment information, or other sensitive information.
