Help with version 1.0

Before contacting support

  • Update Squish Up and Google Play Games to their latest available versions.
  • Check your internet connection if ads, consent, sign-in, or the leaderboard does not load.
  • Restart the Game and your device.

Google Play Games sign-in

Leaderboard sign-in is optional. Confirm that the intended Google account is active in Google Play Games. Squish Up never asks for or receives your Google password.

Scores and leaderboard

A leaderboard score requires an internet connection and Google Play Games sign-in. Locally displayed progress and achievements do not automatically sync to the cloud.

Progress and achievements

In version 1.0, achievements and game progress are stored only on your device. Clearing app data or uninstalling the Game may permanently remove this local progress.

Advertising and privacy choices

Advertising is provided through Google AdMob. Where available, consent choices may be reviewed from the Game's privacy options. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Contact

For additional help, use the developer contact shown on Squish Up's Google Play Store listing. Include your app version, Android version, device model, and a short description of the problem. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment details, or other sensitive information.
