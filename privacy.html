Eksen Works · Squish Up

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Eksen Works handles information in connection with Squish Up on Android and iOS (the “Game”). No account is required for core gameplay. Eksen Works does not operate a game backend and does not ask you to provide your name, postal address, phone number, or payment information.

Information stored on your device

Squish Up stores settings, scores, achievements, earned Squish Coins, cosmetic selections, pending leaderboard scores, and other progress locally. A custom photo you select is processed into an app-private image on your device. Squish Up does not upload the selected photo to an Eksen Works server. Platform backup or device-transfer services may copy eligible local data according to your device and account settings.

Advertising and consent

Squish Up uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements and Google’s User Messaging Platform to request consent where required and provide privacy choices where applicable. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and ad mode, Google and its advertising partners may process IP-derived approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, product interactions, advertising data, crash logs, performance data, and consent information to deliver, limit, personalize, measure, and protect ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

Game services

Game Center on iOS and Google Play Games on Android are optional and used for authentication and leaderboards. The applicable platform provider may process a player identifier, display name, authentication status, scores, and ranks under its own privacy policy. Core gameplay remains available without leaderboard sign-in.

Sharing

When you choose to share a score card, Squish Up creates an image locally and opens the system share interface. Only the destination you select receives that content. The destination handles it under its own privacy practices.

Purchases

Squish Up does not offer in-app purchases. The Game is free and supported by advertising.

Your choices and retention

You can manage consent where the in-app privacy-options control is available, decline optional rewarded ads, avoid leaderboard sign-in, choose whether to select or share a photo, and reset or remove local data. Local information remains until you reset it, clear application data, or uninstall the Game, subject to platform backup behavior. Service providers retain information under their own policies and controls.

Children’s privacy

Squish Up does not require an account and does not ask users to submit personal contact information. Advertising and consent behavior depends on the age, audience, and regional settings configured with the platform and advertising providers.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit the Squish Up Support page or email eksenworks@gmail.com. Do not include passwords, authentication codes, payment information, or other sensitive information.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the Game, its service providers, or applicable requirements change. The effective date will be revised when an update is published.
