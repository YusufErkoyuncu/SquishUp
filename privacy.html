Squish Up

Privacy
Policy.

Effective date: August 25, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Eksen Works handles information in connection with the mobile game Squish Up (the “Game”). We aim to keep the Game simple and do not ask you to create an account or directly provide your name, email address, postal address, or phone number to play.

Information processed automatically

The Game may use third-party services that automatically process limited technical and usage information. Depending on the Game version, your device, and your privacy choices, this may include:

  • device and advertising identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device model, operating system, language, and app version;
  • ad impressions, interactions, and related diagnostics; and
  • crash or performance information.

Eksen Works does not use this information to identify you by name. Third-party providers process information under their own privacy policies and may act as independent data controllers in some regions.

Advertising

Squish Up may use Google AdMob to display advertising. When advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may process device information and advertising identifiers to deliver, measure, prevent fraud in, and improve ads. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your location, age-related settings, consent choices, and device settings.

You can learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and on Google’s How Google uses information from sites or apps page.

How information is used

Information processed through the Game may be used to:

  • operate, maintain, and improve the Game;
  • display and measure advertising;
  • understand performance and fix technical problems;
  • protect the Game and advertising ecosystem from fraud or abuse; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

Legal bases and your choices

Where required by law, processing is based on your consent, the legitimate interests of operating and securing the Game, or compliance with legal obligations. Consent choices may be presented in the Game. You can also limit ad tracking or reset your advertising identifier through your device settings. Available rights vary by region and may include access, deletion, correction, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent.

Data retention and security

Eksen Works retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above. Third-party providers set their own retention periods. We use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children’s privacy

The Game does not ask players to submit personal contact information. Where age-related laws apply, advertising and consent settings are intended to follow the applicable platform and provider requirements. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us through the support route below so the matter can be reviewed.

International processing

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their privacy policies explain the safeguards they use for international data transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game, its services, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an update is published.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, visit the Eksen Works support page. Please do not include passwords, precise location, government identifiers, payment information, or other sensitive personal data in a support report.
