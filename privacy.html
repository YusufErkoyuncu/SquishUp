Squish Up

Privacy
Policy.

Effective date: August 28, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Eksen Works handles information in connection with the mobile game Squish Up (the “Game”). No account is required to play. The Game does not ask you to directly provide your name, postal address, phone number, or payment information.

Information processed automatically

Depending on your device, region, Game version, consent choices, and use of optional features, the Game and its service providers may process:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device, advertising, app-instance, and other identifiers;
  • device model, operating system, language, app version, and network information;
  • app interactions, ad impressions, ad interactions, and gameplay-related events;
  • diagnostics, loading times, responsiveness, battery use, and other performance data; and
  • if you use Google Play Games: your Play Games player identifier, display name, authentication status, leaderboard score, and rank.

Advertising and consent

Squish Up uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display and measure advertising and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and store consent choices where required. Google and its advertising partners may use the information described above to deliver ads, measure performance, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized according to your consent choices, device settings, age-related settings, and applicable law.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps.

Google Play Games and leaderboard

Google Play Games is optional for core gameplay. The Game can recognize an existing Play Games session, and provides a manual sign-in option. Google Play Games processes gamer identity and game-service information to provide authentication and leaderboards. Squish Up reads the player identifier and display name and sends leaderboard scores; it displays leaderboard names, scores and ranks. Other players' access to your Play Games information depends on your profile visibility and Google's service settings. Eksen Works does not receive your Google password. You can manage or delete Play Games data using Google's Play Games and account controls.

Squish Up stores settings, achievements, earned Squish Coins, cosmetic selections and other game progress locally. The Game does not use Google Play Games cloud saved games. Android's backup and device-transfer features may copy selected settings, progress, pending leaderboard scores and earned-reward recovery data, depending on your device and backup settings, and may restore them when you reinstall or change devices. Clearing app data or uninstalling the Game does not necessarily delete platform backup copies. You can manage available backup controls in your Android and Google account settings. Leaderboard scores and ranks held by Google Play Games are managed separately through your Play Games account and settings.

Sharing a score card

When you choose to share, Squish Up creates a game image in the app's local cache and opens Android's sharing interface. The selected destination app receives the image and share text with a temporary file-access permission. Squish Up does not upload that image to an Eksen Works server. The destination app handles the shared information under its own privacy policy.

Google Play purchases and Remove Ads

Squish Up offers an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase through Google Play. It removes interstitial advertising; optional rewarded ads for Continue, Double Reward and Skin Trial remain available and remain subject to applicable privacy choices. Google Play displays the price and processes payment under its own terms and privacy policy. Squish Up does not receive your payment-card number or bank details.

The Game uses Google Play purchase information, including the product, purchase status and a purchase token, to recognize, acknowledge and restore the purchase. The Game keeps a local ownership setting for offline use. The current implementation handles purchase tokens in memory, does not store order IDs or payment details in its own purchase cache, and excludes that cache from Android backup and device transfer. Google Play maintains its own purchase records. Restore and reconciliation contact Google Play when available; offline status can reflect the last successfully known ownership state. You can manage purchases and available refund options through Google Play. Removing local app data does not delete Google's purchase records.

Purposes

  • provide, maintain, and improve the Game;
  • display, personalize where permitted, and measure advertising;
  • provide optional Google Play Games sign-in and the leaderboard;
  • provide, recognize, and restore the optional Remove Ads purchase;
  • understand app interactions, diagnose service problems, and improve performance;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be transmitted to Google and its partners when required to provide AdMob, UMP, Google Play Games, and Google Play Billing features. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies and may act as independent controllers in some regions. Eksen Works does not sell personal information for money.

Your choices

You may decline optional Google Play Games sign-in and continue using gameplay features that do not require the leaderboard. Where available, you can review or change advertising consent in the Game. You may also manage ad personalization, reset or delete your advertising identifier, and manage Play Games data through your Google and device settings. Rights vary by region and may include access, deletion, correction, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent.

Retention and security

The Game keeps local settings and progress in app storage. Temporary share images are stored in the app's cache. You can clear local app data through Android settings; doing so can remove your local progress. Backup copies and data held by Google Play Games, Google Play Billing, or advertising services are separate from local app storage and are managed through the relevant platform controls and provider policies. We do not promise a fixed deletion period for data retained by those providers. The Game uses Google's SDKs for its online services; their documented transport protections apply to those connections. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children's privacy

The Game does not ask players to submit personal contact information. Age-related advertising, consent, and platform settings are intended to follow applicable laws and provider requirements. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information may contact us through the support page.

International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than where you live. Their privacy policies describe applicable international-transfer safeguards.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the Game, its services, or legal requirements change. The effective date will be revised when an update is published.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, visit the Eksen Works support page. Please do not include passwords, precise location, government identifiers, payment information, or other sensitive data in a support request.
